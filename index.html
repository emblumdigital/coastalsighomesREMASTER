Our Story

With over 20 years active in the construction industry, Coastal Signature Homes brings extensive expertise in residential building, home remodeling, and project management. Based in Fort Myers and serving all of Southwest Florida including Cape Coral, we strive for perfection so that excellence is always attained.

From new construction to full home remodels, our team delivers precision, efficiency, and the highest quality standards on every project. Whether it's a kitchen remodel, bathroom transformation, or complete new build, we approach every project with the same commitment to outstanding craftsmanship and client satisfaction.
